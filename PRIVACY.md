# Affinity MCP Privacy Policy

**Language: English | [简体中文](PRIVACY.zh-cn.md)**

This policy applies to unmodified versions of the Affinity MCP plugin distributed with this file and maintained by [Paloexiz](https://github.com/Paloexiz), including the local proxy, connection checker and bundled Skill. The project repository is [Paloexiz/Affinity-MCP](https://github.com/Paloexiz/Affinity-MCP). Contact us about privacy through [project Issues](https://github.com/Paloexiz/Affinity-MCP/issues). “We” means the project maintainer.

This is an independent community project. Affinity by Canva, Codex, ZCode, Claude Code, your chosen AI model services and GitHub have their own providers and privacy policies. This policy describes the plugin's and maintainer's practices and does not replace those policies.

## 1. Local connection and AI clients

The proxy runs on your computer and connects to Affinity's MCP service at `http://localhost:6767` by default. It forwards tool requests from your client to Affinity and returns results to the client. The maintainer operates no plugin backend receiving this runtime data. The plugin has no built-in advertising, usage analytics, telemetry, tracking cookies or maintainer account system.

**A local connection does not make the entire workflow offline. Document text, script output and canvas previews returned to your client may be sent to your chosen AI model service and recorded or retained by that service.** Training use, retention and processing countries depend on the client, provider, account plan and settings. Review those rules before using personal information, confidential files or third-party work.

## 2. Information the plugin processes

The information depends on the tool calls you or your agent initiate:

| Information | Purpose and destination |
| --- | --- |
| Tool names, arguments, script code, file paths and document or object identifiers | Passed from the client through the proxy to Affinity to locate objects and carry out requests. |
| Document text, object properties, file information, script results and errors | Returned by Affinity to the client; may include personal information or content printed by scripts. |
| Canvas or selection previews | Generated by Affinity and returned to the client; may show a full page, a selection and sensitive content within it. |
| SDK documentation, library scripts, task hints and issue reports | Read, saved, searched or submitted as requested; storage and recipients are described below. |
| Protocol and plugin versions, request correlation information and connection diagnostics | Used to initialize connections, match responses and troubleshoot failures. |

The proxy does not independently scan your disk or upload documents in bulk. Scripts can access files, modify documents or use networks within Affinity's permissions and actual SDK capabilities. The proxy does not redact returned content. The Skill guides agent behavior; it is not an enforced permission check or content filter.

## 3. Storage, logs and retention

- **Proxy runtime data:** requests, response buffers and connection state are processed in memory. The proxy creates no document database, call archive or separate log files. Associated pending state is cleared when a request ends, and process memory ends with the process; this is not a promise of secure erasure or deletion of copies saved by other software.
- **Client output and diagnostics:** results and errors go to the client through standard output. Diagnostics such as discovery failures go to standard error. Upstream errors may contain paths, addresses or other original information. Your client, terminal, operating system or log redirection may retain this material according to their settings.
- **Connection checker:** when you run it, it reads the selected installation's manifests and proxy file, discovers online tools and reads the SDK preamble, which may contain hints saved by the app. Its summary prints the installation path, configuration, versions, proxy hash and tool count; failures produce diagnostics. It does not automatically send these to the maintainer.
- **Affinity content:** saved documents, exports, library scripts and local task hints are managed by Affinity or your file system until you remove them or the app clears them under its rules. Uninstalling the plugin does not automatically delete them.
- **Support material:** when you submit an Issue, we receive your public GitHub account information and submitted content to answer you, troubleshoot and maintain the project. Issues and necessary technical discussion may remain as public project history. Unneeded personal details are deleted or redacted from copies we control, except where legally required, and kept only as long as necessary.

## 4. Optional features and third-party recipients

- **Script networking:** after you enable Affinity's network permission and run a relevant script, the websites or services specified by that script may receive requests and their data.
- **Script library and hints:** relevant tools trigger script reads, script saves and local hint saves. Hint sharing and Canva AI features are governed by Affinity's settings and service rules. Hints, inputs and generation requests may leave your device; local hint storage and hint sharing are separate features.
- **SDK issue reports:** calling `report_sdk_issue` submits the description and any supplied code through Affinity. The bundled Skill requires prior explicit authorization; the proxy does not enforce that check. Review and remove sensitive content before submission.
- **Downloads, updates, web pages and support requests:** these connect to GitHub, a plugin marketplace or the relevant website, which processes network and account information under its own policy. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

The plugin does not automatically collect your documents for the maintainer's advertising, data sales or model training. Sending content to an AI service, Affinity, a script's destination or a public Issue follows the separate paths above. Recipients may process data outside your country or region; the plugin makes no promise about third-party data residency.

If you change `AFFINITY_MCP_BASE_URL` to a remote address, requests and results go to that configured service. The proxy has no built-in authentication or additional transport encryption layer; the default HTTP connection is unencrypted. Configure a remote connection only with authorization to use that recipient and appropriate protections in place.

## 5. Your choices and rights

You can approve or reject tool calls in your client, disable or uninstall the plugin, and turn off MCP, file access, networking, hint sharing and other optional permissions in Affinity. Denying a permission may prevent its associated feature from working. Provide only what a task needs and inspect previews and logs before sharing them.

Where applicable law grants rights to information, access, correction, deletion, restriction, withdrawal of consent or other rights, contact us through [project Issues](https://github.com/Paloexiz/Affinity-MCP/issues) about information the maintainer actually holds. We will verify and handle requests as required by law, requesting only the minimum information needed to locate and verify a request. You may also complain to the competent data protection authority.

**Issues are public. Do not post identity documents, credentials, unredacted logs, private files or sensitive personal information.** If private communication is needed, first post only a request to establish contact and wait until an appropriate channel has been agreed before providing necessary details. For records independently held by GitHub, AI providers or Affinity, also use that provider's rights channels. We cannot directly access or delete their independently held records or guarantee removal of public content copied or cached by others.

## 6. Security and children

Protect your computer, client accounts and document backups, and enable only permissions needed for your task. The plugin provides no independent access control, sensitive-content detection or backup recovery service and cannot guarantee risk-free scripts or third-party processing. If information held by the maintainer is affected by a security incident, we will take remedial and notification steps required by law.

The plugin is not directed to children and does not solicit children's personal information. Follow applicable law and client age requirements when minors are involved. Contact us through the channel above to request deletion if the maintainer receives children's information that should not be processed.

## 7. Policy changes

This policy accompanies the corresponding plugin version. When data practices change, we will update the policy and describe the change in the project changelog. Updating a file or continued use does not replace any separate notice or consent required by law. Consult the policy accompanying your version for its description; modified versions and services hosted by others should explain their own practices.
